## Indexing lane

The single serialized executor for every indexing job inside a vault runtime (SPEC-0104 US3). The lane acquires `.rhizome/index.lock` for indexing and validation projection jobs, with `Kind.LockRole()` recorded in it. Interactive repairs keep their validate-owned lease and ask the lane to yield.

- **Entry points**: `New(Options)` returns the real `Executor`; `Lane.Submit`/`Lookup`/`Status`/`Close`, `Handle.Subscribe`/`Cancel`, `Reporter` and its optional `SummaryReporter` extension, `Kind`, `Event`; `Fake` for tests.
- **Key invariants**: one worker goroutine, one job at a time; explicit index jobs preempt a running background job by cancelling its context; background work checks cancellation between bounded work units and drains before releasing its lease; background kinds run FIFO; cancelled queued jobs complete immediately without waiting for another job or priority hold; explicit requests always coalesce and may join a *running* job, background submitters only join a *queued* one so their change always gets a pass that starts after they called; the lane never retries a cancelled job — its submitter requeues the pending work; per-handle event history is bounded (512, newest kept) and replayed on Subscribe before live events, with non-blocking drop-oldest fan-out that always delivers `EventDone`; `Lookup` keeps the last 32 finished jobs; an external priority request cancels a running background job within one second and holds background scheduling (`Status.Held`) until it clears; explicit jobs are never interrupted by external priority; rebuild is never a lane job (`ErrRebuildNotAllowed`). Database identity preflight runs before waiting for the writer lease and again after acquisition, so a replacement during contention cannot reach the job body.
- **Boundary**: job bodies come from `pkg/app/bootstrap` (watcher batch, boot catch-up) and `cmd` (explicit index); the lane owns ordering, the lock, preemption, and event fan-out only.

- **Cancellation boundary**: a priority poll requests cancellation within one second; lease release follows only after job work returns and required durability drains finish. Boot validation participates in that cooperative cancellation.

- **Expected skips**: a job may wrap `ErrSkipped` when its durable work became unnecessary. Its diagnostic report and terminal diagnostic event are INFO `skipped/job_skipped`; the handle error and subscriber outcome retain their existing error semantics so the submitter still controls recovery.

- **Diagnostics ownership**: unique admissions create a bounded collector and operation before queueing. Only diagnostic identity and recorder propagate onto the lane's own cancellation; the collector is owned by that execution. Coalesced callers add their operation/trace identity to a join event without creating a second report. Terminal reports include queue/lease/execution waits and safe outcome reasons after active work drains. Publication precedes `EventDone`; subscribers arriving during finalization still receive it, and publication failure cannot suppress completion. Optional summaries render only after the operation total span closes. Explicit/boot index reports alone can update `latest-index`; watcher/validation reports remain separate.
