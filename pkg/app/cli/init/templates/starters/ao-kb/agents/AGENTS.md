## AO KB knowledge

- Use `ao-kb-setup` to install, check, or recover the AO KB knowledge workspace.
- Change knowledge through `/ao-kb:process`, `/ao-kb:curate`, or `/ao-kb:suggest`.
- AO-owned paths are listed in `.rhizome/source-ownership.yml`; Rhizome refuses native edits to them. Do not edit or delete that file to get around a refusal.
