## Complex Domain

- Use the complex-domain workflow when product behavior depends on domain concepts, processes, workflows, sources, or traceable requirements.
- Treat complex-domain notes as upstream evidence and constraints for spec-driven delivery. Specs remain the delivery contract.
- Reuse a known anchored pack. For a topic-only request, run bounded `domain-inventory-pack` queries for relevant live types before optional semantic `domain-topic-survey` candidate ranking.
- Distinguish unresolved anchors, missing authored coverage, degraded retrieval, capped results, and stale evidence. Do not claim complete coverage from a partial result.
- Keep requirements atomic and keep `candidate` distinct from `accepted`. Provenance, links, confidence, and semantic similarity do not authorize acceptance.
- Preserve source identity, version history, prior cited evidence, and exact locations. A source/code disagreement needs evidence before either is declared wrong or stale.
- Keep `DomainProcess` for business/system operation and `UserWorkflow` for actor-facing tasks and variants.
- Keep authored links, readable targets, full-obligation satisfaction, and authorized lifecycle updates separate. Partial delivery does not make an entire requirement implemented.
- Use `ActionItem` for ambiguity, conflict, missing source evidence, or another unresolved accountable decision without inventing an assignee.
- Compose with the `rhizome` skill for session, authoring, mutation, and proportionate validation mechanics, and with Agentic Engineering for spec, effort, alignment, reconciliation, and completion authority.
