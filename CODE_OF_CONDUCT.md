# Code of Conduct

Treat people with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. Critique ideas and code clearly, accept good-faith disagreement, and make room for people with different experience and backgrounds.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Do not bring private client or personnel information into project discussions.

Atomic Object maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect a constructive project environment. They may decline contributions or end a discussion that no longer helps the project.

Raise concerns privately with a maintainer through an available private contact channel. If none is available, ask for a private contact in an issue without naming affected people or describing sensitive details. Reports are considered as maintainer capacity allows; this policy does not promise an investigation or response deadline.
